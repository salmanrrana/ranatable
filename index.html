RANATABLE

a spectral instrument played entirely with your hands

◍ Open hand  →  play. Move left/right for pitch, up/down for glow. Close your fist for silence.

◍ Two hands  →  lead voice + warm lower harmony.

◍ Pinch a glyph in the dock  →  pull it onto the table. Where you place it shapes the sound.

◍ Pinch a placed glyph and twist your wrist  →  turn it up or down.

◍ Drag a glyph back into the dock  →  it dissolves.

◍ Choose Aether, Velvet, Glass, or Ember with the Sound menu or glyph.

needs a camera · headphones recommended · everything stays on your device
For steady tracking, light your hands evenly and keep fingers in view.
